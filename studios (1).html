Where Actors Train

Most actors take classes to get better at acting. Los Angeles has a lot of acting studios that teach different skills like comedy, drama, and acting on camera.

The table below shows some popular acting studios in LA and what they are known for.

List of Studios

Studio Area Known For
The Groundlings Hollywood Comedy and improv
Playhouse West North Hollywood Meisner technique
Howard Fine Studio Hollywood Scene study
Ivana Chubbuck Studio West Hollywood Emotional acting
